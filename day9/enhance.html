Nutrition Sources
NutriScope's data, calculation methods, and reference standards.
📚
All nutrient data is derived from publicly available Indian and international food composition databases. Values are per 100g of edible raw food unless otherwise stated. Nutrient content varies by cooking method, food variety, and growing conditions.
📊
Data Sources & References
1
ICMR-NIN Nutritive Value of Indian Foods (2017)
Primary source for macronutrient and micronutrient composition of Indian foods. Covers 528 food items with values per 100g edible portion. Indian Council of Medical Research, Hyderabad.
2
ICMR-NIN Recommended Dietary Allowances (RDA) for Indians (2020)
Age- and gender-specific RDAs used for target calculations. Covers protein, energy, vitamins (A, B-complex, C, D, E, K), minerals (iron, calcium, zinc, iodine, magnesium).
3
FSSAI Dietary Guidelines for Indians (2022)
Food Safety and Standards Authority of India guidelines on balanced diets, food group portions, and health promotion used for recommendation logic.
4
WHO Global Nutrient Reference Values
Used for Vitamin D (15 µg/day), Vitamin B12 (2.4 µg/day), and upper intake levels for sodium and saturated fat where ICMR values are not available.
5
Mifflin-St Jeor Equation (1990)
BMR calculation formula validated across Indian populations. Males: (10×W)+(6.25×H)−(5×A)+5. Females: (10×W)+(6.25×H)−(5×A)−161. TDEE = BMR × activity factor.
6
USDA FoodData Central (supplementary)
Used for foods not covered in ICMR-NIN database and to cross-validate Vitamin D and B12 values for animal-derived foods and fortified products.
🧮
Calculation Methods
⚡
Energy Targets
TDEE adjusted by goal: Maintain (±0), Lose Weight (−300 kcal), Gain Muscle (+300 kcal). Macro split: 45% carbs, 25% protein, 30% fat of total calories.
🥩
Protein Target
1.6g per kg body weight — in line with ICMR-NIN's recommendation for active adults. Increased to 1.8g/kg for muscle gain goal.
⚖️
Micronutrient Targets
Gender-specific RDAs from ICMR 2020. Iron: 8mg (male), 18mg (female, non-pregnant). Calcium: 1000mg adults. Pregnancy condition applies elevated targets (iron 27mg, calcium 1200mg, folate 600µg).
🎯
Risk Scoring
Composite risk score (0–100) derived from weighted deviation of key risk nutrients from their targets. Chronic conditions adjust nutrient weightings. Not a validated clinical tool.
